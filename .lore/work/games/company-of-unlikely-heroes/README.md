# Barnaby's Order of Last Resort history has graduated

The game formerly called The Company of Unlikely Heroes continues in its
[private standalone repository](https://github.com/rjroy/order-of-last-resort),
including its game-specific research and art history. Access requires permission
to that repository.

The public source history at the graduation snapshot remains available at
[Mind Garage commit 64e6289](https://github.com/rjroy/mind-garage/tree/64e6289821b912f394e4eaa744ae76e0ce485a3f/.lore/work/games/company-of-unlikely-heroes).
The standalone [issue mapping](https://github.com/rjroy/order-of-last-resort/blob/master/.beads/MIGRATION.md)
connects transferred Mind Garage Beads IDs to their new IDs.
