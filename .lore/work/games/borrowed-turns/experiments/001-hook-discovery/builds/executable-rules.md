# Borrowed Turns: executable prototype rules

These are provisional AI-selected details for the authorized first playable comparison, not playtest findings or a claim of originality. The originating proposal, Ronald's contributions, and six hypotheses remain in `record.md` and `capture-bargains.md`. All variants use the same encounter and independently selectable base and Invert-preview modes. There is no progression gate, random number generator, or external service. The player wins by escaping, not by killing every pursuer.

## Board and actions

Coordinates are integer `[x,y]`, x increasing right and y increasing down; cardinal adjacency and distance use Manhattan distance. The production starting board is 7 by 7, no walls, player `[1,3]` with 4 HP, exit `[6,3]` opening on turn 4. Enemies, in resolution order: `a` at `[4,1]`, 2 HP, `bolt` aimed down `[0,1]`; `b` at `[4,5]`, 2 HP, `shove` aimed up `[0,-1]`. The exit is traversable before opening. These are provisional content choices, not unit-test constants. Enemies stand still and announce the same action/direction each turn while alive. Every announced ray extends up to three cells, stopping before a wall or board edge. The ray includes every cell up to that boundary and affects every actor in it, in increasing distance from origin; if tied, player precedes enemies in fixture order. An actor cannot occupy another actor's cell. A wall blocks rays; actors do not. The originating actor is never affected. Enemy attacks can hit other enemies.

The player has exactly one ordinary action each turn: `move` one unoccupied, in-bounds, non-wall cardinal cell; `wait`; `capture`; `release`; or `discard`. No basic attack. The target of a release is a cell, not an actor: it must be in bounds, 1–3 cells cardinally from the player's current cell, with no wall through and including it. Its ray ends at the selected cell. Empty targets are legal. Bolt deals 1 damage to every affected actor. Shove deals 1 damage then pushes each surviving affected actor one cell away from the origin, if free and in bounds; otherwise it stays. Invert preview changes shove's push to a pull one cell toward origin, with the same blocking rule. Shove is the only reversible action. Push/pull displacement is processed immediately after each actor's damage; actors newly entering the ray are not added to its initially collected targets. HP reaching zero removes an enemy immediately, or ends the run as failure for the player. Never reduce HP below zero.

Capture selects an alive enemy's currently announced, not-yet-cancelled action, within Manhattan distance 3, regardless of walls, and requires an empty slot. It cancels only that enemy's current occurrence, not future announcements. A captured action retains kind and source ID, not the enemy's direction. Release uses the player's current origin and selected target, consumes the slot before applying effects, and consumes the ordinary action. Discard consumes the slot and the ordinary action. Neither requires an enemy to remain alive except V4. `invert` is an optional boolean on release, legal only in enabled preview mode and for shove; it is not a separate action or permanent toggle.

## Turn and ordering

Initial turn is 1 and all alive enemies announce. A successful ordinary action resolves immediately. On a move or release, resolve all effects first. If the player is alive on the exit and the current turn is at least the opening turn, win immediately, before enemies; a player killed by their own release fails instead. Otherwise, in fixture order, each surviving enemy whose occurrence was not captured resolves its announced ray. Stop resolving immediately on player death. Resolve any V4 source loss immediately on enemy death, including enemy friendly fire. After enemy resolution, if alive, process V3 expiry or V5 backlash, then failure if backlash killed the player. Finally increment turn and announce fresh occurrences for surviving enemies. Invalid commands do not spend an action, consume free capture, change events, or alter any state. Terminal states reject all commands.

The slot lifetime is counted in successful ordinary player actions *after* capture; invalid commands and V5's free capture itself never count. The slot is usable on the second such action, then expires/backlashes at that action's end if still present. Capturing as an ordinary action does not count itself. V5's mandatory ordinary action on the same turn as free capture **is the first subsequent action**. To preserve the later-turn promise, release on the capture turn is illegal in every variant, including V5. V5 discard is legal on that turn and immediately backlashes before enemy resolution. For V5 discard or deadline, bolt and shove each deal 1 direct damage to the player, with no push/pull and no ray or LOS; this is their explicit backlash. Backlash consumes the slot first. If backlash or ordinary action kills the player, failure stops further resolution. Escape on the final action takes precedence over end-of-turn expiry/backlash because the run ends immediately.

## Bargains

- **V1 Spend a Turn:** capture is the ordinary action, indefinite slot, discard costs the action.
- **V2 Close Approach:** capture is the ordinary action and source must be adjacent after an optional atomic one-cell cardinal approach. Approach must be free, in bounds, and end adjacent; if already adjacent, omit `approach`. A rejected capture never moves the player. Other rules are V1.
- **V3 Brief Possession:** V1 plus expiry after the second subsequent ordinary action; release on that action is legal. Expiry is harmless.
- **V4 Keep the Source Alive:** V1 plus slot is destroyed immediately if its source dies before release. Release consumes the slot before its effects, so killing its own source with that release succeeds.
- **V5 Store the Consequence:** one free capture per turn while empty, then an ordinary action; no replacement of an occupied slot, even if free capture remains. Discard or holding through the second subsequent action triggers backlash as above. Releasing on that second action avoids it. The once-per-turn flag resets only when the turn advances.
- **V6 Show Your Hand:** V1 plus capture atomically commits a target **cell** legal from the capture position under release geometry. Later movement or obstacles may make release invalid; it must use that exact cell, never track an actor or silently retarget. Reposition or discard. The capture target is validated before any state mutation.

## Model contract

`res://model/encounter.gd` is a `RefCounted` script instantiated with `.new()`. `configure(fixture: Dictionary, variant: String, invert_enabled: bool) -> void` initializes an independent run; valid variants are `V1` through `V6`. `reset() -> void` restores a deep copy of the configured initial fixture, variant and preview mode. `snapshot() -> Dictionary` returns a deep, JSON-friendly copy. Callers cannot mutate model state through inputs or outputs. `act(action: Dictionary) -> bool` applies a legal action atomically and returns true; illegal actions return false unchanged. `assess(action: Dictionary) -> Dictionary` returns `{ "legal": bool, "reason": String, "preview": Dictionary }`, never mutates anything, and uses empty reason on success. An illegal action returns a nonempty human-readable reason and empty preview; a legal preview is exactly the complete snapshot that applying the action would produce, including ordered events. This supplies actual resolution/geometry to the UI rather than a parallel implementation. `ray` events show only rays that actually resolve; they are not complete telegraphs if an earlier effect ends the run. Current complete telegraphs come from `intentions` instead. Assessing is not an action and consumes nothing.

Fixture schema: `{width:int, height:int, walls:Array[[int,int]], player:{pos:[int,int], hp:int}, exit:{pos:[int,int], opens:int}, enemies:Array[{id:String, pos:[int,int], hp:int, kind:"bolt"|"shove", dir:[int,int]}]}`. Fixtures are valid, have unique enemy IDs and nonoverlapping actors, positive HP and board size, cardinal enemy directions, and a walkable exit. Each call configures from fixture data, not production-level constants. Enemy kind and direction never change. Valid commands are `{type:"wait"}`, `{type:"move", to:[x,y]}`, `{type:"capture", source:id}` with optional `approach:[x,y]` for V2 and required `target:[x,y]` for V6, `{type:"release", target:[x,y], invert:bool}` where V6 target may be omitted or must equal commitment, and `{type:"discard"}`. Omitted `invert` means false. Unsupported keys and malformed commands are rejected; approach is allowed only in V2 and target only in V6 capture.

Snapshots contain `turn`, `status` (`playing`, `won`, `lost`), `variant`, `invert_enabled`, `player`, `exit`, `width`, `height`, `walls`, `enemies` (alive only, fixture order, with original fields), `cancelled` (IDs captured this turn, reset each turn), `free_capture_used` (boolean), `stored` (empty `{}` or `{kind, source, captured_turn, actions_elapsed, target}` where target is `[]` except V6), `intentions`, and `events` (ordered events from the latest successful command, initially empty). `intentions` is an array in fixture enemy order, containing `{source: String, kind: String, cells: Array[[int,int]]}` for every alive enemy with an announced, noncancelled occurrence in the current turn. Cells are its complete ordered ray under the board geometry, regardless of whether an earlier enemy would end the run. In a terminal snapshot `intentions` is empty. Immediately after V5 free capture, omit its cancelled source while retaining all other alive announcements; after the turn advances, list fresh announcements. Free V5 capture is a successful command but not a turn and gets its own events; following action replaces those events. Snapshot contains no shared mutable references.

Events are dictionaries with `type` and specified fields, no extra event types required: `move` `{actor, from, to}`; `capture` `{source, kind}`; `release` `{source, kind, target, invert}`; `discard` `{source}`; `ray` `{actor, kind, cells}` (ordered cells); `damage` `{actor, amount, hp}`; `displace` `{actor, from, to}` only on successful movement; `death` `{actor}`; `slot_lost` `{source, reason}` where reason is `source_dead` or `expired`; `backlash` `{source, kind, amount}`; `turn` `{number}`; `win` and `loss` with no additional fields. Actor is `player` or enemy ID. Emit release before its ray; emit backlash before its damage; emit damage before death; emit death before source-loss; emit outcome at the point specified above; emit `turn` after all end-of-turn effects. A cancelled or dead enemy emits no ray. V2 approach emits move then capture. Effects of a ray are based on occupants collected at ray start, not re-collected as actors move.

## Scope of evidence

Deterministic test-owned fixtures and checks are specified in `games/borrowed-turns/TESTING.md`. They establish rules correctness, not enjoyment, balance, or originality. The parent experiment owns comparison criteria, telemetry consent, manual UI verification, and replay manifests. No human play or screening result is asserted here.
