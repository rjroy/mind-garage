# One Borrowed Turns position

This is an optional paper turn from a reachable Switcher + B fight. No need to launch the game. If you want to try it without hints, read this before the research reports.

```text
      x 0 1 2 3 4 5 6
    y
    0   . . . . . . .
    1   . . . . . . .
    2   . . . A . . .
    3   . . * * . . .
    4   . B * P . . .
    5   . . * * . . .
    6   . . . . . . .
```

You (`P`) have 6 HP and 3 AP. Both enemies have 5 HP. No walls. `*` and your current square are in B's announced shove.

- You hold Swap from A. For 1 AP, Shine exchanges you with an enemy up to two cells away in a straight line, then deals that enemy 1 damage. Stepping one square costs 1 AP. Ending the turn is free.
- A resolves first. It has announced a Swap on `[3,4]`: it exchanges with the occupant, if any, then deals 1 damage.
- B resolves second. Its shove hits the two columns immediately to its right, three cells wide, for 1 damage and a push. If B moves before its attack, the attack starts from its new position. Enemies move normally after both attacks resolve.

**What would you like to make happen?** Pick a line and predict who ends the attacks hurt or unharmed. A safe line is a legitimate choice. Before checking anything, notice whether you wanted to try another line or just wanted the answer. There’s no score to report.
